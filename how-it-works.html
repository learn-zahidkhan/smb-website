How it works

A simple, private and respectful process in five steps.

  1. Get in touch

    Message us on WhatsApp at +91 73042 64698 or fill the online registration form. Start with a free 15-minute consultation. We respond during working hours, 10 AM to 7 PM, all days.

  2. Share your preferences

    Tell us about the candidate, the family, education, profession and expectations. Biodata and photos are collected privately.

  3. Receive a shortlist

    We share suitable, checked profiles, only with your consent. Nothing is ever posted publicly.

  4. Meet the family

    If both families are interested, we arrange and coordinate the meeting.

  5. Marriage

    We stay with you through to the engagement and nikah.

Documents to bring

Biodata with family details, 2 recent photos, ID proof copy, education and job details.

Your privacy at every step

Address and phone numbers stay private until both families agree to meet. You can ask us to remove your profile at any time.
