About Shagufta Marriage Bureau

Bringing families together with respect and trust since 2024.

Our story

Shagufta Marriage Bureau was started in Mumbai in 2024 with a simple belief: a marriage is not just between two people, it is between two families. Finding the right match needs patience, honesty and personal care, not just a list of profiles.

Shagufta Khan started SMB because she believes matchmaking should go beyond photographs, biodata and superficial expectations. For her, the right match brings emotional security, mutual respect, companionship and happiness.

Every proposal is handled personally, with attention to family background, education, profession, values and preferences. Every profile is kept confidential, and every family is treated with the respect it deserves.
