Frequently asked questions

Answers about registration, privacy, documents, fees and timings.

Who can register with Shagufta Marriage Bureau?

Families or individuals looking for a suitable life partner can register. The candidate must meet the legal marriage age in India: 18 for women and 21 for men.

How do I register?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 73042 64698 or fill the online registration form. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We never publish profiles on the website or social media. Profiles are shared only with suitable families, and only with your consent.

What details or documents are needed?

Biodata with family details, 2 recent photos, an ID proof copy, and education and job details.

What are the registration charges?

The registration fee is ₹5,000 and is non-refundable. Full payment is ₹30,000. We explain everything in your free consultation. See Services.

How long does it take to find a match?

Every family's requirements are different, so the time varies. We focus on sharing suitable profiles rather than many profiles.

Do you only serve families in Mumbai?

We are based in Mumbai and serve families in Mumbai and beyond, including NRI families in the Gulf and abroad.

Can parents register on behalf of their son or daughter?

Yes. Many registrations are done by parents or relatives. We also ask the candidate to confirm their own consent before the profile becomes active.

What are your working hours?

We are available from 10 AM to 7 PM, all days of the week.

Can I remove my profile later?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will remove your details from our records.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp. We reply between 10 AM and 7 PM, every day.
