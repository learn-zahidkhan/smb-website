Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Shagufta Marriage Bureau ("SMB", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information through this website and our WhatsApp service, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

Details you submit in our registration form or share with us on WhatsApp: name, gender, age, city, education, profession, partner preferences and contact number. After your consultation, we may also collect biodata, family details and photographs that you choose to share.

2. Why we collect it

Only to provide matchmaking services: to understand your requirements, contact you, and share your profile with suitable families.

3. Consent

We use your information only after you give consent. When a parent or relative registers on behalf of a candidate, we also ask the candidate to confirm their own consent. You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Sharing

We never publish profiles on this website or social media. We share profile details only with families whose requirements match, and only with your permission. Address and phone numbers are shared only after both families agree to meet. We do not sell your data.

5. Storage and security

Form submissions are stored securely with our website and data service providers, with access limited to our team. We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for matchmaking, or until you ask us to delete it. Payment records are kept as required by tax law.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 73042 64698.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.

10. Contact for privacy questions
