Meet the Founder

Shagufta Khan

Founder, Shagufta Marriage Bureau. Guiding families in Mumbai since 2024.

Guided by trust. United by values.

“A successful marriage is not about finding a perfect person. It is about finding someone with whom you can build a beautiful life.”
Shagufta Khan

Shagufta believes that matchmaking should go beyond photographs, biodata and superficial expectations. Every family that comes to SMB is heard personally, and every proposal is handled with care, patience and confidentiality.

She respects parents' wishes while making sure the bride and groom have their own say, because marriage is a lifelong commitment, not a race against time.

Mohd Aslam Khan, Co-Founder

Aslam ji supports families with coordination, follow-ups and family meetings, so the process stays smooth and comfortable. Like Shagufta ji, he believes every individual deserves an opportunity to be understood.
